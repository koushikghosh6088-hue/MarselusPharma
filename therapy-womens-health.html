Women's Health

PCOS management, fertility support, and metabolic balance with inositol therapy and Vitamin D supplementation.

Overview

Addressing PCOS & Metabolic Balance

Polycystic Ovary Syndrome (PCOS) affects a significant proportion of women of reproductive age. Beyond its reproductive implications, PCOS is associated with insulin resistance, metabolic dysfunction, and increased long-term cardiovascular risk.

Marselus offers targeted solutions combining physiological inositol ratios with Vitamin D supplementation, addressing the hormonal, metabolic, and nutritional dimensions of PCOS.

Scientific Rationale

Physiological Ratio

The 40:1 ratio of Myo-Inositol to D-Chiro Inositol mirrors physiological parameters to optimize ovarian function.

Insulin Sensitisation

Synergistic components support healthy insulin transduction, glucose utilization, and cardiovascular health.
