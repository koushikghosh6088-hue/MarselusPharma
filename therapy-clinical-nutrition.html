Clinical Nutrition

Comprehensive immunonutrition solutions supporting recovery, immune function, and nutritional rehabilitation.

Overview

The Role of Immunonutrition

Clinical nutrition plays a vital role in patient recovery, particularly in post-surgical settings, critical illness, and conditions with increased metabolic demand. Immunonutrition — the targeted supplementation of specific amino acids and micronutrients — has been shown to modulate immune function and improve clinical outcomes.

Marselus addresses this need through IMMUNOMARS, a comprehensive immunonutrition sachet designed to support the body's natural defence and recovery mechanisms.

Scientific Rationale

Immunomodulation

Targeted L-Glutamine, L-Lysine and L-Arginine support cell regeneration, metabolic pathways and immune responses.

Oxidative Defense

A robust micronutrient blend containing Vitamin C, Zinc, and Selenium assists physiological tissue defense.
