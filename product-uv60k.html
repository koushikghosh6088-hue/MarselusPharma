What is UV 60K?

UV 60K is a high-dose Cholecalciferol (Vitamin D3) 60,000 IU softgel capsule indicated for the prevention and treatment of Vitamin D deficiency across multiple clinical contexts.

UV 60K is positioned across three approved indication contexts, each addressed independently below. These contexts must not be merged into a single claim set.

Indication Contexts

Gut Immunity

Vitamin D plays a critical role in maintaining gut barrier integrity and modulating the immune response within the gastrointestinal tract. Deficiency is commonly observed in patients with inflammatory bowel disease (IBD) and other gastrointestinal conditions.

Key Points

  • Supports gut barrier function and mucosal immunity
  • Modulates inflammatory pathways in the GI tract
  • Correction of deficiency in IBD and malabsorption syndromes
  • Complementary support alongside standard gastroenterology therapy

Women's Wellness & Fertility

Vitamin D deficiency is highly prevalent among women with PCOS and has been linked to impaired insulin sensitivity, irregular ovulation, and suboptimal fertility outcomes. Supplementation with Cholecalciferol may support hormonal balance and reproductive health.

Key Points

  • Supports hormonal balance in women with PCOS
  • May improve insulin sensitivity and metabolic markers
  • Complementary support for fertility outcomes
  • Addressing widespread Vitamin D deficiency in women

Related Products

SARANYA

Bone & Muscle Health

Cholecalciferol is essential for calcium absorption and bone mineralisation. High-dose supplementation is indicated for the prevention and treatment of osteomalacia, osteoporosis risk reduction, and musculoskeletal health in Vitamin D-deficient populations.

Key Points

  • Supports calcium absorption and bone mineralisation
  • Prevention and treatment of osteomalacia
  • Risk reduction for osteoporosis
  • Supports musculoskeletal strength and function

Key Information

Composition

Cholecalciferol (Vitamin D3) 60,000 IU per softgel capsule.

Dosage

As directed by the physician. Dosing schedule depends on severity of deficiency and clinical context.

Formulation

Softgel capsule for enhanced absorption and patient convenience. Stable formulation preserving potency.
