What is IMMUNOMARS?

IMMUNOMARS is a comprehensive immunonutrition sachet combining key amino acids (L-Glutamine, L-Lysine, L-Arginine) with essential micronutrients (Multivitamins, Zinc, Selenium, Vitamin C) to support immune function, recovery, and overall nutritional status.

Designed for patients requiring nutritional support during recovery, illness, or periods of increased nutritional demand.

Indications

  • Immune support and recovery
  • Post-surgical nutritional supplementation
  • Clinical nutrition support in illness
  • Periods of increased nutritional demand

Key Information

Composition

L-Glutamine, L-Lysine, L-Arginine, Multivitamins, Zinc, Selenium, Vitamin C — presented as a sachet for oral use.

Suggested Dose

1 sachet per day, dissolved in 150–200 ml of water, for 7–14 days or as advised by the physician.

Administration

Dissolve contents of one sachet in 150–200 ml of water. Stir well and consume immediately after preparation.

Important Safety Information — Renal Impairment Caution

Use with caution in patients with renal impairment. Amino acid supplementation (particularly L-Arginine and L-Glutamine) requires dose adjustment or monitoring in patients with compromised renal function. Consult the prescribing physician before use in patients with known renal insufficiency.

Scientific Evidence

Immunonutrition with amino acids and micronutrients has demonstrated benefits in supporting immune function and recovery in clinical settings. L-Glutamine is well-documented for its role in gut barrier integrity and immune cell function.
