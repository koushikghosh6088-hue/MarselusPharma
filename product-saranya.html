SARANYA

Myo-Inositol + D-Chiro Inositol + Chromium Picolinate + Vitamin D2

Hormonal & Metabolic Balance for PCOS

Request Information View Monograph

What is SARANYA?

SARANYA is a scientifically formulated combination of Myo-Inositol, D-Chiro Inositol, Chromium Picolinate, and Vitamin D2 designed to support hormonal and metabolic balance in women with Polycystic Ovary Syndrome (PCOS).

The physiological 40:1 ratio of Myo-Inositol to D-Chiro Inositol works synergistically to support insulin sensitisation, ovulatory function, and reproductive health.

Indications

  • Support for hormonal balance in PCOS
  • Metabolic balance and insulin sensitisation
  • Support for ovulatory function and fertility

Key Information

Composition

Myo-Inositol, D-Chiro Inositol (40:1 ratio), Chromium Picolinate, Vitamin D2.

Verify against approved label.

Dosage

As directed by the physician. Treatment duration to be individualised based on clinical assessment.

Target

Women with PCOS — supporting hormonal balance, metabolic health, and reproductive wellness.

Content Note

Metformin-comparison and clinical-advantage claims are pending approval and have not been included. Metformin is not included in the composition. This content will be updated once Marselus medical/regulatory sign-off is received.

Scientific Evidence

The combination of Myo-Inositol and D-Chiro Inositol in the physiological 40:1 ratio is supported by growing clinical evidence for improving insulin sensitivity, menstrual regularity, and ovulatory function in women with PCOS.

Clinical study references pending regulatory approval for publication.
