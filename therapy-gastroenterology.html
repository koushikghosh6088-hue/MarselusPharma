Gastroenterology

Evidence-based therapies for IBD, Ulcerative Colitis, and gut health — combining anti-inflammatory agents, immunonutrition, and targeted Vitamin D support.

Overview

The Gastrointestinal Challenge

Inflammatory Bowel Disease (IBD), including Ulcerative Colitis, represents a growing clinical challenge with significant impact on patient quality of life. Effective management requires targeted anti-inflammatory therapy, nutritional support, and correction of common deficiencies.

Marselus Pharmaceuticals addresses these needs with a complementary portfolio of evidence-based treatments spanning mesalamine therapy, immunonutrition, and Vitamin D supplementation.

Scientific Rationale

Targeted Action

5-ASA (Mesalamine) remains the first-line therapy for mild-to-moderate UC, delivering anti-inflammatory action locally at the colonic mucosa.

Barrier Integrity

Gut mucosal integrity is strongly supported by functional amino acids and Vitamin D supplementation, preventing bacterial translocation.
